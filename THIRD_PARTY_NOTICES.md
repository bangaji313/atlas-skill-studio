- **Academic Research Skills / ARS-Codex:** CC BY-NC 4.0. Public source availability does not grant unrestricted commercial use.
- **Hypit:** modified Apache-2.0, including branding/notice retention and conditions on commercial redistribution and multi-tenant/hosted services. Consult the pinned upstream LICENSE; Atlas does not grant exceptions.
- **OpenMontage:** AGPL-3.0 and embedded component notices. Its full pinned source and the Atlas patch instructions are available through the lockfile. Derivative distributions must preserve applicable obligations.
- **Remotion:** its own license, including eligibility and company-license conditions. The collection supplies guides, not a license to use a renderer outside its terms.
- **Media:** stock music, sound effects, fonts, textures and sample imagery may have terms independent of the code repository. The public repository does not mirror those media libraries. The source installer retrieves upstream files locally; that is not permission to redistribute or use every asset commercially. Video Shotcraft’s historical first BGM item lacks precise track provenance in upstream attribution and should be replaced or separately verified before use.
- **Optional services:** API/model providers and applications have their own terms, account permissions and usage charges.

Review the exact pinned component LICENSE and asset notices for your intended use. When updating a source lock, review changed terms along with the code. Do not add a blanket MIT badge to assembled studio packages.
